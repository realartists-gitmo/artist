# Lexicon packages

Lexicon data is auxiliary metadata, not the prose interpreter and not a completeness requirement.

A lexical entry may own language, lemma, part of speech, forms, and sense IDs. A lexical sense may own a definition, semantic target, usage scope, evidence, and sense-to-sense relations. Predicate frames may record syntactic realizations, candidate semantic arguments, ontology-selection hints, voice/constructions, or attestations.

No lexicon can enumerate all prose, so `muse-semantic-label-6` retains exact source-anchored open lexical identity. That does not make lexicon/ontology use optional. Pinned entries and frames are deterministic semantic evidence: when they license a narrower ontology concept or participant relation in context, the label uses it. Unknown vocabulary still receives the narrowest justified foundational ontology type; lexical anchoring preserves the residual word-level identity. Frames may never override contradictory source evidence or manufacture unsupported world knowledge.

Attestations are typed evidence for usage, not ontological truth. Lexicon acceptance remains evidence-backed governance metadata and cannot override the frozen label contract.
